# Mectronics ARASM Website

A responsive conference website developed for **ARASM**, providing information about the conference, programmes, speakers, research activities, paper submission, and related resources.

## 📌 About the Project

The Mectronics ARASM website is designed to provide a centralized online platform for presenting conference-related information and resources.

The website contains sections for:

- Conference information
- Programme details
- Research and academic activities
- Speaker information
- Paper submission resources
- Conference-related documents
- Images and media
- Information about Madurai and its attractions

## 🎯 Objectives

- To provide a simple and user-friendly conference website.
- To present conference programmes and important information clearly.
- To provide easy access to paper submission documents and resources.
- To showcase speakers, research activities, and conference-related content.
- To organize images, documents, and other resources in a structured manner.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Website structure and content
- **CSS3** – Styling and page layout
- **JavaScript** – Client-side functionality
- **Images and Multimedia** – Conference and location-related visual content
- **PDF/DOCX Resources** – Conference and paper submission documents
